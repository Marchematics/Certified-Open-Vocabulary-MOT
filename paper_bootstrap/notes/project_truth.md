## Venue Style

## Dominant Contribution Type

Framework / governance paper with empirical validation.

The paper should be positioned as release-time governance for frozen scientific candidate universes under scarce one-sided verification, not as a new generator and not as prospective materials discovery.

## Central Claim

PARC treats publication/release itself as the statistical object: a finite candidate set can be released, refused, or routed to audit under a stated false-release target when only one-sided verification is available.

## Evidence Hierarchy

Primary strict headline:

- CTC K=100 active audit: 0.5% top-score audit, 20/20 safe seeds, 2000 releases, 0 false releases, matched-budget random 0/20, full random audit 200x larger.

Clean-acceptance support:

- T1 empirical baseline frontier: 11 method families, materials fixed-budget utility, and explicit materials validation no-go ledger.

Boundary support:

- refusal attribution, audit uncertainty intervals, materials source-discordance diagnostics, contamination sensitivity diagnostics.
