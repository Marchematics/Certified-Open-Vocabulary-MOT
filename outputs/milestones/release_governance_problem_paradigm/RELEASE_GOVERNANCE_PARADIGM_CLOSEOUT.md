# Release-Governance Problem Paradigm

Status: completed paper-facing synthesis package.

This package implements the route-2 positioning: the paper should be read as release-time governance under scarce one-sided verification. It binds CTC active-audit evidence, the T1 empirical baseline frontier, refusal attribution, and audit uncertainty into one manuscript-facing closure. It does not add new experimental labels and does not promote materials diagnostic routes into positive evidence.

## Components

- problem_definition: One primary headline, one T1 baseline frontier, and scoped boundary diagnostics are linked in the evidence hierarchy. (problem-frame only; does not assert a new materials discovery result)
- ctc_active_audit_primary_anchor: 20/20 safe seeds; 2000 releases; 0 false releases; 200x budget ratio versus full random audit. (completed simulated-audit strong positive for CTC only)
- t1_empirical_baseline_frontier: Empirical frontier includes 11 method families: Bao-style selective conformal adaptation, PARC, calibrated threshold, e-BH-style, fixed threshold, nnPU classifier release, oracle prefix, post-filter e-value, raw top-K, raw top-R, split conformal candidate threshold. (target-object-aware baseline frontier; not equality of baseline guarantees)
- materials_fixed_budget_frontier: ALIGNN K=300 raw top-K FTR 0.253; PARC FTR 0.087; raw top-R matched FTR 0.087; unstable follow-ups prevented 64.25. ALIGNN K=500 raw top-K FTR 0.327; PARC FTR 0.048; raw top-R matched FTR 0.048; unstable follow-ups prevented 158.30. (retrospective public-label utility; raw top-R separates smaller queue effect)
- materials_validation_boundary: 5/5 materials independent/prospective validation routes are not positive evidence. (do not claim independent or forward-looking materials validation success)
- refusal_attribution_closure: 5 refusal rows have failure-mode attribution. (refusal attribution diagnostic; not a new release-positive row)
- human_audit_uncertainty_boundary: 2 audit interval rows with Clopper-Pearson, Wilson, and Jeffreys intervals. (audited boundary support; external labels remain pending where not returned)

## Required Main-Text Discipline

- CTC active audit is the only primary strict headline.
- T1 is the empirical baseline frontier and clean-acceptance support.
- Materials are retrospective public-label release-policy evidence, not a forward-looking validation claim.
- Refusal attribution is part of the governance result, but remains diagnostic rather than a new positive release row.
