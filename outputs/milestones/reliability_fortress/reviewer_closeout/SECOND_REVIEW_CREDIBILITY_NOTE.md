# Second-Review Credibility Note

The current release includes three second-review evidence blocks:

- The original 300-row second-review table, retained as a protocol reference.
- A hard-case Boundary-500 challenge with 25 disagreements out of 500 rows, label agreement 0.95, and Cohen's kappa 0.8325676092.
