# Abstract Claim Scope

This file defines allowed and disallowed abstract-level language for the public artifact package.

## Allowed Broadness Language

- PARC converts frozen ranked candidate lists into auditable release-or-refuse decisions.
- Completed evidence supports strict CTC release certification, fixed-budget materials public-label utility, and real human-audited release/refusal behavior.
- Materials fixed-budget rows may be described as public-label computational follow-up or retrospective public-DFT utility.
- External-source materials joins may be described as source-discordance stress tests.

## Disallowed Oversell Language

- Do not claim prospective materials discovery from A1/A2 or A3 unless the A3 DFT gates are met.
- Do not describe OQMD or alex-mp diagnostics as positive independent validation.
- Do not describe CGCNN K=100 as the paper-facing utility primary unless a separate predeclaration artifact exists.
- Do not imply experimental synthesis, universal materials discovery, or model training improvement.

## A3 DFT Gate

The paper does not claim prospective materials discovery unless all of the following are true: `released_n >= 25`, `selection_frozen == true`, `dft_completed_n >= 25`, and `primary_FTR <= alpha`. Until then, MatterGen/A3 rows are pending or diagnostic only.
